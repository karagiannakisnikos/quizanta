Quizanta is a trivia quiz game for Android with over 11,000 questions in English, and thousands more in Greek and in Spanish, in 17 categories, from History and Science to Maps, Flags and Emoji Riddles. New: Visual Puzzles, endless shape puzzles: what comes next, count the triangles, mirror images and memory. Play solo or Guess the Number, play the Daily quiz (the same 10 questions for everyone, every day) and keep your streak going, play live with friends on their own phones, take turns on one phone, or send a challenge link to friends anywhere.
